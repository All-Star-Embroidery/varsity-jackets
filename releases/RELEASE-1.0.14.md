# All Star Varsity Jackets v1.0.14

## School-first jacket product identity

- Makes the **school name** the primary large heading on dedicated varsity jacket product pages.
- Moves the current **jacket style name** into a smaller subtitle directly beneath the school name.
- Adds a compact **Jacket Styles** switcher underneath the mascot/location metadata when a school has multiple linked styles.
- The current style is clearly marked and the other styles link directly to their dedicated varsity product pages.
- Adds block controls to show/hide the same-school style switcher and customize its label.
- Keeps the responsive medium, scale-to-fit gallery from v1.0.13.
