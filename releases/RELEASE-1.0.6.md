# All Star Varsity Jackets v1.0.6

## Browse by School compact layout

- Moved **Available Customizations** into the school/style sidebar directly underneath the jacket style selector.
- Removed the customization panel as a separate grid item, which was forcing the Browse by School stage into an unnecessary second row and making the section much taller than its content required.
- Narrowed the school/style sidebar slightly so the selected jacket gallery receives more horizontal space.
- Tightened the school-stage gap, divider spacing, and customization spacing for a more compact desktop presentation.
- Restyled Available Customizations as a compact sidebar list while preserving all five customization categories and their descriptions.
- On tablets and phones, customization options become a horizontal, swipeable row beneath the style choices so they do not create another tall stack before the jacket gallery.
- Reduced excess vertical spacing around the school carousel/stage without making the controls feel cramped.

## Goal

Browse by School should feel like one focused product-selection viewport: choose a school, choose one of the typically 2–3 jacket styles, immediately see a large gallery, and still have customization information visible without the entire section becoming oversized.

## Compatibility

- WordPress 6.4+
- PHP 8.0+
- WooCommerce integration supported when WooCommerce is active
- Uses the organization-owned updater at `All-Star-Embroidery/varsity-jackets`
